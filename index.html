          📘 How to Use TutaMaps

          🌟 Built for Pedestrians in Coimbatore

          Standard navigation forces pedestrians onto dangerous, unshaded highways or dark alleys. TutaMaps is built specifically for walking people, mapping every small street, footway, shop, and emergency amenity without requiring any API keys.

          🧭 How Search & Navigation Works:

          • Single Search Bar: Starts clean and minimal. Tap it to enter start and destination or pick popular destinations.
          • Two Route Comparison: Route 1 gives shade canopy and water points; Route 2 shows the direct shortcut.
          • Live Navigation: Tap ▶ Start Walking to see minutes to go, live street changes, and current place updates.

          ☀️ Heat Mode vs 🌙 Night Mode:

          • ☀️ Heat Mode: Protects against Coimbatore's 40°C+ summer heat. Prioritizes tree shade and hydration points.
          • 🌙 Night Mode: Prioritizes brightly illuminated avenues, open shops, and active footfall.
          • 🚶 Normal Mode: Balanced pedestrian routing with safe sidewalks and crosswalks.

          🏥 Hospitals, Water ATMs & Restrooms:

          • 🏥 Hospitals: 24/7 casualty trauma centers and primary health centres.
          • 🚰 Free Drinking Water: Corporation RO water dispensers and Panchayat water ATMs.
          • 🚻 Public Restrooms: Swachh Bharat public convenience blocks.
