          How We Calculate Walk Score (Weighted Methodology)

          Our TutaMaps application calculates a Walk Score (0 to 100) for every available walking route using a weighted scoring system based on five core factors: safety, shade or tree cover, lighting, water availability, and route directness.

          General Calculation:
          Walk Score = ∑ (Factor Score × Factor Weight)
          Final score is represented on a clean scale of 0 to 100.

          📊 How Factor Weights Vary Across Modes:

          The same route receives different Walk Scores in different modes because the importance of each factor changes to reflect pedestrian needs:

          Evaluation Factor 🚶 Normal Mode ☀️ Heat Mode 🌙 Night Mode
          🌿 Shade or Tree Cover 0.20 (20%) 0.45 (45%) ⭐ 0.10 (10%)
          💡 Street Lighting 0.20 (20%) 0.05 (5%) 0.35 (35%) ⭐
          🛡️ Pedestrian Safety 0.25 (25%) 0.15 (15%) 0.30 (30%)
          🚰 Water Availability 0.15 (15%) 0.25 (25%) 0.10 (10%)
          🧭 Route Directness 0.20 (20%) 0.10 (10%) 0.15 (15%)
          Sum of Factor Weights 1.00 (100%) 1.00 (100%) 1.00 (100%)
          1. 🚶 Normal Mode (Balanced Approach)

          Considers all five factors evenly to recommend a route with good overall walking conditions, balanced between comfort, speed, and safety.

          2. ☀️ Heat Mode (Shade Weight: 0.45)

          Gives greater importance to shade and tree cover (weight: 0.45) and water availability (0.25) because pedestrians need protection from direct sunlight and heat exhaustion.

          3. 🌙 Night Mode (Lighting Weight: 0.35)

          Gives greater importance to street lighting (weight: 0.35) and pedestrian safety (0.30) because visibility and ambient vigilance are essential when walking after dark.

          💡 Core Principle: "In simple words, we don't change the destination; we change what matters most when selecting the walking route." TutaMaps compares all available routes and recommends the route with the highest score for the selected mode.

          How TutaMaps Works

          🌟 Built for Pedestrians

          Standard navigation algorithms are tuned for cars, forcing pedestrians onto baking 40°C highways or dark alleys. TutaMaps maps small footways, sidewalks, shade canopy, and emergency facilities.

          🧭 Key Capabilities:

          • 🌿 Time-Aware Shade Engine (SunCalc): Calculates real solar altitude and shadows to recommend tree-shaded paths.
          • 🚰 Emergency Amenities: Maps drinking water ATMs, 24/7 casualty hospitals, pharmacies, and restrooms along your route.
          • ☀️ Heat Mode & 🌙 Night Mode: Optimizes routes for afternoon sun protection or well-illuminated evening walking.
          • 🚶 Multiple Candidate Paths: Compare multiple pedestrian routes to choose your preferred path.
